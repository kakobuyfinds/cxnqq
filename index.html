From discovery to decision

How to use a KakoBuy spreadsheet

A spreadsheet organizes your research. Confirm the exact listing and available buying route before placing an order.

01 / DISCOVER

Open the source

Choose a find and open its CAFANS product page. Match the product reference, images and selected version rather than relying on a similar name.

02 / COMPARE

Check your options

Record the size, color, measurements and seller description. If a detail is missing, ask for confirmation before paying.

03 / PLAN

Confirm the buying route

Review the platform choices on the source page. If KakoBuy is available for that item, check its current price, fees and order requirements there.

04 / REVIEW

Inspect before shipping

Where available, review photos of your actual order, confirm quantities and measurements, and resolve issues within the applicable return window.

Plan beyond the item price

Build a realistic shopping budget.

A low listing price is only one part of the total. Compare the full amount for the same product option, destination and delivery service.

  • Product cost: exact size, color, quantity and listing currency.
  • Service costs: confirmed payment, handling or agent charges.
  • Shipping: domestic delivery and international delivery, including packaging where charged.
  • Other charges: any applicable taxes, duties or carrier fees shown in your quote.

Unknown charges should remain open questions. A zero entered below does not mean a charge will be waived.

What to check before you buy

Keep the original listing beside your notes so you can revisit the same product and option. Separate seller claims from details you have confirmed.

CategoryRecord in your shortlistResolve before payment
ShoesSize system, selected size, foot measurements and listing reference.Ask about fit and relevant measurements. Foot length and insole length are different measurements.

Helpful answers

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a KakoBuy spreadsheet?

A KakoBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize finds, source links, prices and buying notes. This independent page presents a sample sourced from CAFANS and practical comparison tools. It is not an official KakoBuy catalog.

Where do the buttons take me?

The “KakoBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons both open https://cafans.com. They do not submit an order or open a checkout. Individual source-product links open the matching CAFANS detail page.

Is this the full CAFANS product directory?

No. The directory above contains three sourced products captured on 19 November 2026. Browse CAFANS for its current selection. Search, category filters and sorting on this page apply only to this sample.

Do the displayed prices include shipping?

No. Displayed USD figures are indicative item prices observed on CAFANS. Shipping, service fees and applicable taxes are separate and must be confirmed before payment. The final charge may differ by option and exchange rate.

Are these product photographs verified QC images?

No. These are product listing images from CAFANS. This page has not independently inspected the products or verified warehouse QC photos. If order-specific photos are available, review the actual item, measurements and selected option before shipping.

Can every product be ordered through KakoBuy?

Availability depends on the particular product, seller and platform. Open the source listing and check its current platform options. Do not assume an item is supported merely because it appears in a product discovery guide.

How should I save products for comparison?

Create one row per product option with columns for name, source URL, category, size, color, original price, currency, estimated shipping, fees, measurements, QC status and last-checked date. Mark unresolved details clearly and refresh your notes before ordering.
